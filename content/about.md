Hi, I'm Devrim. I work in information security in Bonn.

Most of my time goes into keeping a company secure in a structured way. I run its information security management, look at risks, and audit organisations against standards like ISO/IEC 27001 and NIS2. I also lead a small penetration testing team, so I stay close to how systems actually get broken into. That helps a lot when the conversation turns to policies and processes.

The part I enjoy most is the space between technology and people: turning technical findings into decisions someone can actually make, and explaining things so they make sense. That's also why I teach. Every winter I run a hands-on course on offensive security, and seeing students get from "what's a shell?" to pivoting through networks never gets old.

My path here wasn't a straight line. I started in cognitive science, moved to data science at EPFL, and ended up in cybersecurity. I think that mix shows in how I work.
