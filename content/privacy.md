## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser type. It does this to deliver the site and keep the service secure. I have no access to these logs and do not use them.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
